Need it for a day? Ask your community.

Sharenest is a borrowing platform for verified communities. Post what you need, and the classmates, neighbours or colleagues who already own it can choose to lend it.

People buy things they only need once

Someone in your community probably owns what you need. The hard part is finding them, asking, and agreeing on terms.

You don't know who has it

Borrowing usually depends on people you already know, so many people end up buying instead.

Informal lending is fragile

Return dates, item condition and who pays for damage are rarely agreed, which leads to misunderstandings.

Open marketplaces don't fit

They help you find items listed for rent. They don't let you ask one trusted community for something specific.

How Sharenest works

Owners never have to list their belongings. They only respond when a request matches something they have and are happy to lend.

  1. Post a request

    Say what you need, when, for how long, and where you can collect it.

  2. Owners respond

    Eligible members of your community or group are notified and can offer to lend.

  3. Agree the terms

    Choose an offer, then both of you confirm the dates, handover and any lending fee.

  4. Return and review

    Confirm the return, then leave feedback tied to the real transaction.

Built for trust inside a community

Verified communities

Access is limited to approved members of a university, hostel, apartment block or workplace.

Request-first borrowing

Ask for what you need instead of searching through listings.

Voluntary lending

Owners respond only when they have the item and are willing. No obligation.

Borrowing agreements

Dates, handover details and any fee are recorded and confirmed by both people.

Condition records

Note the item's condition at handover and return, with photos if needed.

Privacy and accountability

Control who sees your requests, keep contact details protected, and report problems.
